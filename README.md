# Objectives
The analysis of pizza sales data focuses on identifying daily and weekly sales trends, highlighting peak hours and slow periods to find opportunities for boosting sales. It also aims to develop strategies to maximize performance during high-traffic times and understand weekly fluctuations to support targeted marketing or promotional efforts.

The analysis of pizza categories and sizes aims to assess their impact on sales, orders, and quantity sold. By identifying popular sizes and high-performing products, marketing can be optimized. Additionally, underperforming items like Brie Carre Pizza are analyzed to uncover causes and guide improvement strategies to boost their sales and revenue contribution.

---

# Insights
This project provides a visual analysis of pizza sales data, highlighting revenue, order behavior, and product performance. Below are the key insights derived from the dashboard:

### 1. Overall Sales Performance

* **Total Revenue:** $817.9K
* **Total Pizzas Sold:** 49.6K
* **Total Orders:** 21.4K
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** $38.3
* **Average Pizzas per Order:** 2.3

Customers usually order more than one pizza per transaction, showing a tendency for group or family purchases.

### 2. Peak Sales Periods

* **Busiest Hour:** 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM (lunch time).
* **Busiest Week:** Week 47 (December), likely due to holiday season.

📌 Promotions can be maximized during lunch hours and festive periods.

### 3. Sales Trends

* **Hourly Trends:** Significant spikes during lunch and evening hours.
* **Weekly Trends:** Orders remain steady, with noticeable peaks in December.

📌 Opportunity to increase sales during evening with targeted promotions.

### 4. Pizza Categories

* **Top Category:** Classic Pizza – 26.91% of revenue (~$220K).
* Followed by Supreme (25.46%), Chicken (23.96%), and Veggie (23.68%).

📌 Classic Pizza contributes most to revenue, total orders, and pizzas sold.

### 5. Pizza Sizes

* **Best Seller:** Large Pizza (L) – $0.38M revenue.
* Followed by Medium ($0.25M) and Small ($0.18M).
* XL and XXL contribute minimally.

📌 Large and Medium pizzas are the core revenue drivers.

### 6. Best & Worst Performing Pizzas

* **Top 5 by Revenue:** Thai Chicken, Barbecue Chicken, California Chicken, Classic Deluxe, Spicy Italian.
* **Top 5 by Quantity/Orders:** Classic Deluxe, Barbecue Chicken, Hawaiian, Pepperoni, Thai Chicken.
* **Bottom Performers:** Brie Carre Pizza (lowest revenue, quantity, and orders), Mediterranean, Spinach Supreme, Calabrese.

📌 Low-performing pizzas should be reviewed for recipe improvement, promotion, or removal.

### 7. Key Takeaways

* Lunch hours and holiday weeks are the most profitable times.
* Classic + Large Pizza is the best-performing combination → can be positioned as a **flagship product**.
* Underperforming pizzas need evaluation to optimize menu offering.
* Evening sales can be further boosted with promotions or meal bundles.

📊 This dashboard highlights valuable business insights that can support decision-making for product strategy, promotional activities, and menu optimization.

# Suggestion
Based on the insights from the Pizza Sales Dashboard, here are several suggestions to improve sales performance and profitability:

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Products

* Highlight **Classic and Large pizzas** as the flagship products.
* Create **combo packages** (e.g., Classic Large Pizza + Drinks + Sides) to boost Average Order Value (AOV).
* Promote **top 5 pizzas by revenue and quantity** through special deals or loyalty programs.

### 2. Improve Low-Performing Products

* Reevaluate underperforming pizzas (e.g., Brie Carre, Mediterranean, Spinach Supreme).
* Options: discontinue, rebrand with new recipes, or introduce seasonal limited editions.
* Offer **trial discounts** or **bundle promotions** to test customer acceptance.

### 3. Time-Based Promotions

* Maximize sales during **lunch hours (12:00 – 1:00 PM)** with “Lunch Specials.”
* Introduce **Evening Deals** (e.g., “Buy 1 Large Pizza, Get 1 Small Free”) to increase dinner sales.
* Launch special promotions during **holiday weeks (e.g., December week 47)** to leverage seasonal demand.

### 4. Size Strategy

* Focus on **Large and Medium pizzas** as they are the top revenue drivers.
* Position **XL and XXL** as premium options with limited availability to create exclusivity.
* Encourage upselling from Small → Medium/Large with minimal price differences.

### 5. Customer Engagement

* Implement a **loyalty program** (e.g., points for every order, free pizza after certain purchases).
* Use targeted marketing to promote best sellers and seasonal offers.
* Collect customer feedback on underperforming pizzas to guide recipe or menu adjustments.

✅ By focusing on best-selling products, optimizing underperforming items, and leveraging time-based promotions, the pizza business can increase revenue, reduce waste, and improve overall customer satisfaction.
